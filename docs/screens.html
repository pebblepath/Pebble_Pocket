Face rules for ages three and up

How it stays usable by someone who cannot read

One shape per face

A single centred object tells the child what this face is for. If a state needs two things on screen, one of them is spoken instead.

Words only when teaching

The only text on any child-facing face is the pair Pebble is teaching. Status, names, titles and errors are all spoken by Pebble or shown to the parent in the app.

Colour is the label

Teal glow: Pebble is listening or talking. Amber: the French word. Terracotta: the one thing that leaves the device, a hello. Parents choose a colour per song, so tiles never need titles.

Big targets, no precision

Every tappable thing is at least 96 px at 1×, about 9 mm. Tiles fill the face in twos. Nothing sits in a corner. Damp thumbs and a bouncing lap still work.

Buttons are the safety net

Press: talk. Hold: hello. Any press from anywhere: home. A child who gets lost in a swipe is one press from the stone.

Nothing to finish, nothing to win

No streaks, stars or timers. A song ends and the tile comes back. A calm loop runs until someone presses. The device never asks the child for anything.
