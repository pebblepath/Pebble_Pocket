Face rules

How the language holds together on 33 mm

One idea per face

Every state has a single headline in Nunito 800 and at most one line of Inter under it. If a face needs a paragraph, it is two faces.

Colour means something

Teal glow is Pebble talking or listening. Amber is the French word (the Language domain colour in the app). Terracotta is the one action that leaves the device: Hello.

Dark, but warm

The app avoids dark mode by default; the wrist is the exception, because black pixels are free on AMOLED. The mesh sheens keep it PebblePath rather than generic-gadget black.

The ripple stone is the only character

No faces, no eyes, no cartoon reactions. Pebble is a stone on still water, the same mark as the app's advisor. It pulses, it glows, it never emotes.

Buttons beat gestures

Two physical buttons carry the core loop: press to talk, hold to send a hello. Swipes are for browsing only, so a three-year-old with damp hands can still use it.

Type sizes, at 1×

Time 104 px · headline 30 px · body 23 px · captions 19 px. Nothing under 18 px anywhere on the face.
